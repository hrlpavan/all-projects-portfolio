## Core Capabilities & Engineering Highlights
- **Direct**: Direct DaVinci Resolve timeline audio track extraction
- **Local**: Local Whisper model inference with zero external cloud cost
- **Automated**: Automated word-by-word subtitle track generation directly into timeline
- **Custom**: Custom text styling and animation preset injection
