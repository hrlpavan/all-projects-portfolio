## Core Capabilities & Engineering Highlights
- **Comprehensive**: Comprehensive trademark protection guidelines across NICE Classes 9, 38, 41, and 42
- **Statutory**: Statutory DMCA Takedown Notice and Counter-Notice protocol (17 U.S.C. 512)
- **Software**: Software licensing doctrine safeguarding proprietary C++, Metal, and CUDA shader IP
- **Interactive**: Interactive Apple HIG-styled legal web portal and printable vector PDF charters
