## Executive Summary
A rapid diagnostic and repair utility that eliminates 'Media Offline' frame drops in DaVinci Resolve by repairing corrupted render caches and relinking external storage paths.

## Core Capabilities & Engineering Highlights
- **Automated**: Automated CacheClip and RenderCache corruption scanner for DaVinci Resolve projects
- **External**: External volume mount-point verification and symlink relinking utility
- **H.264/HEVC**: H.264/HEVC VideoToolbox hardware decoder reset scripts for macOS
- **Safe**: Safe non-destructive backup of Resolve project database before cache purge
