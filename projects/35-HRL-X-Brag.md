## Core Capabilities & Engineering Highlights
- **Programmatic**: Programmatic 1920x1080 motion graphics renderer with cubic-bezier and back-out easing curves
- **Automated**: Automated multi-segment neural voiceover synthesis and frame-accurate audio-visual mastering
- **Procedural**: Procedural ambient soundscape mixing and dynamic HUD telemetry overlays
- **Turnkey**: Turnkey codebase-to-launch-video pipeline for rapid product showcases
